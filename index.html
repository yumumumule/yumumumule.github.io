About

I study AI systems, from the experiments that help us understand them to the engineering that makes them work.

I’m pursuing a BEng in Artificial Intelligence: Systems & Technologies at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, with expected graduation in 2029. Alongside my studies, I conduct undergraduate research in the AI Robotic Perception Lab under Prof. Hongliang Ren.

01Independent project

Movie recommendation system ↗

Explicit-feedback recommendation with five rating predictors and biased matrix factorization implemented directly in NumPy.

  • Mini-batch SGD, validation-based selection, and cold-start fallbacks.
  • Saved model artifacts, a recommendation CLI, and full-catalogue Top-10 evaluation.
Recorded result

Test RMSE 0.8578 vs. 1.0389 for the global mean on one MovieLens split. Better rating accuracy did not translate into better Top-10 ranking than the constant-score baselines.

02AIST1000 seminar project

Background sensitivity in CLIP ↗

An inference-only study of how object-centric preprocessing affects CLIP predictions under paired background interventions.

  • Frozen CLIP ViT-B/32 across 370 Oxford-IIIT Pet images and 11 conditions.
  • Segmentation-based crops, matched controls, and class-stratified paired bootstrap intervals.
Recorded finding

Original-image accuracy was 83.78% for full images and 83.24% for object crops. Both paired intervals for flip-rate reductions included zero; a robustness benefit remains unresolved.

03ESTR2018 course project

Exploration & exploitation ↗

A probabilistic study of stationary Bernoulli bandits, linking concentration bounds to simulated regret.

  • Uniform random, fixed ε-greedy, and UCB1 policies implemented in NumPy.
  • Independent random streams, paired trials, regret scaling, and Hoeffding experiments.
Experimental scope

The main comparison uses 300 trials of 5,000 rounds. Saved trial data and uncertainty intervals support comparisons; finite simulations do not establish asymptotic regret rates.

Maze Explorer ↗

A C11 terminal game with procedurally generated mazes, limited visibility, and shortest-path hints.

  • Seeded depth-first maze generation and breadth-first pathfinding with wrapping portals.
  • Game-state logic, energy constraints, and a native Windows console interface.
Engineering focus

Separates maze logic from terminal rendering. Includes CMake builds and logic tests covering 1,200 seeded mazes; the repository records Windows verification.
